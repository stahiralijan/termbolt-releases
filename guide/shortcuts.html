💡 Productivity Tips ​

  • Auto-Copy on Select: When enabled in Settings, selecting any terminal text automatically copies it to your clipboard.
  • Double-Click Snippets: Double-clicking any snippet in the retractable sidebar runs the command immediately in the active session.
  • Keystroke Broadcast: Click Broadcast in the session bar to mirror keyboard input across all open server tabs simultaneously.
  • Always-on-Top: Click the Pin icon on the frameless titlebar to keep TermBolt above all other application windows.
